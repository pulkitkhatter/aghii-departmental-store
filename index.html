Quick answers

Can you deliver a gift to someone else?

Yes. Put their address in the cart and the card says who it's from. Add the recipient's phone in the address box.

How do I pay?

Cash or UPI on delivery. Nothing to pay while ordering.

Can I change what is inside a ready hamper?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp after adding it. We swap items at the same price band.

How fast is delivery?

Within about 30 minutes in nearby sectors. Bigger or custom hampers may need a few hours, we confirm on WhatsApp.
